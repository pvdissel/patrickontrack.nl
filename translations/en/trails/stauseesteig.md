The StauSeeSteig is the Dutch Mountain Film Festival's second long-distance trail, opened in October 2024 as the successor to the [Dutch Mountain Trail](/en/trails/dutch-mountain-trail/): longer, harder, quieter and wilder. It runs 125 km just across the border, from Langerwehe station in Germany to Dolhain station in Belgium, with 2,600 metres of climbing and past seven reservoirs.

You walk through the notorious Hürtgenwald, over spectacular dams and through the Eifel National Park, then across the boggy, bleak High Fens to the old fortified town of Limbourg. On the way there are endless forests, steep rocky slopes, fast-flowing streams and deep, dark valleys.
