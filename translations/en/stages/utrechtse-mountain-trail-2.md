From the edge of Amersfoort across the Treekerheide to the Bergzicht pancake house near Woudenberg. The flattest stage of the trail.

**Note:**

- After the Waterlooweg (km 1.9) dogs are not allowed. The official site describes two alternatives: along the bridleway, or past the campsite.

**Food and drink:** [Restaurant Zuiver](https://www.restaurantzuiver.nl/amersfoort/) (km 0.0), [Pannenkoekenhuis Bergzicht](https://pannenkoekenhuizen.com/bergzicht/) (km 11.4).

**Places to stay:** [Landgoed ISVW](https://www.landgoedisvw.nl/kamers) (km 0.0, 800 m off the route), [Buitenplaats Elisabeth](https://www.buitenplaatselisabeth.nl/) (km 11.4, 500 m off the route).

More about this stage, with a video and the route in Komoot: [stage 2 on utrechtsemountaintrail.nl](https://utrechtsemountaintrail.nl/etappe2/).
