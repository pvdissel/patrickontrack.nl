Through the Rhenen town woods and the floodplains to the Grebbeberg, with a wide view over the Nederrijn, then past the military cemetery and Ouwehands Zoo to Rhenen station. According to its makers, by far the hardest stage.

**Note:**

- Dogs are not allowed in the Palmerswaard (km 5.2). Alternative: 100 m further along the Paardenmarkt to the car park, then 300 m south across the grass.
- Dogs are not allowed on the south side of the Grebbeberg either (km 7.9). Alternative: 1.4 km further east along the Cuneralaan.

**Food and drink:** [House of Bird Kwintelooijen](https://www.houseofbird.nl/kwintelooijen/) (km 0.0), [De Thijmse Berg](https://www.thijmseberg.nl/brasserie) (km 2.4), [Moeke](https://www.moeke.nl/rhenen) (km 6.2), [Restaurant Magnolia](https://www.paviljoen.nl/restaurant-magnolia-rhenen/) (km 11.7, 600 m off the route), [Grand Café Cunera](https://grandcafecunerarhenen.nl/menu/) (km 13.0).

**Places to stay:** [B&B Trouwborst](https://www.trouwborst.com/) (km 7.7), [Hotel 't Paviljoen](https://www.paviljoen.nl/) (km 11.7, 600 m off the route).
