---

Part 4 of the guidebook, about 23.5 km. From Mheer you descend into the Meuse valley and cross the river on the ferry. Past the Lanaye lock you climb to **d'n Observant** on the Sint-Pietersberg, the seventh and last of the Seven Summits. From there it's a short way to Maastricht station.

**Note:**

- If the ferry isn't running there is a detour; it's on the DMFF's updated map.
- In 2021 Natuurmonumenten declared part of the Observant's slopes a no-go zone. The route was moved and the description to and from the summit improved.

The updated maps are PDFs on the [official page](https://www.dmff.eu/dutch-mountain-trail/#opmerkingen).
