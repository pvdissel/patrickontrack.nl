start_transport: Hoge Veluwe National Park entrance, Hoenderloo

Straight through the Hoge Veluwe National Park to Arnhem, with a chance of red deer, mouflon and sand lizards. Around km 1 there's an owl box put up by Roots.

**Note:**

- When the water is high (around km 3.5), follow the cycle path straight on from km 2.5 until you meet the route again at a wide sandy track, and turn right there.

Route description, map and current route changes: [stage 20 on Wandelnet](https://www.wandelnet.nl/wandelroute/1342/overzicht).
