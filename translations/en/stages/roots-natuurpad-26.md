A long stage through the Maashorst nature area to Uden, with Exmoor ponies, Kempen heath sheep and Tauros cattle. At the end you cross the Slabroek wildlife bridge.

Route description, map and current route changes: [stage 26 on Wandelnet](https://www.wandelnet.nl/wandelroute/1351/overzicht).
