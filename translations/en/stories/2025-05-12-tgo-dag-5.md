This is an episode of my [TGO Challenge 2025](https://www.tgochallenge.com): solo, self-supported and on foot across Scotland. The plan, following the [Scottish National Trail](https://www.scottishnationaltrail.org.uk): from Fort Augustus to Garva Bridge, 28 km and 730 m of ascent. I walked on to Spey Dam and camped there.
