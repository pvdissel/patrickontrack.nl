This is an episode of my [TGO Challenge 2025](https://www.tgochallenge.com): solo, self-supported and on foot across Scotland. The plan, following the [Scottish National Trail](https://www.scottishnationaltrail.org.uk): from Kingussie to Glen Feshie, 20 km and 300 m of ascent. I started in Newtonmore.
