On 21 August 2025 we went to Fulufjället National Park near Särna, to hike to the highest waterfall in Sweden: Njupeskär. We followed the 3.9 km [Njupeskärsleden](https://www.visitdalarna.se/en/do/activities/njupeskarsleden-39-km). An easy but beautiful walk.

The video is a silent hike: beautiful scenery and music, as if you are walking there yourself.
