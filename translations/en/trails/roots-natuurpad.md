The Roots Natuurpad was laid out by the nature magazine Roots; Wandelnet publishes the stages and the route changes. In 32 stages of 8 to 24 km it runs from Delfzijl, on the edge of Groningen, to Goirle, on the border between Noord-Brabant and Belgium. The route seeks out nature wherever it can: about 70% is unpaved.

On the way you walk over the Hondsrug and through the Drentsche Aa area, past dolmens and over the Drenthe heaths, along the Vecht and the IJssel, right across the Veluwe and through the Hoge Veluwe, over the big rivers and through De Maashorst and the Kampina into Brabant. Every stage has a few spots where Roots points out plants or animals to look for.

## Stages and GPX

The GPX files come from [Wandelnet](https://www.wandelnet.nl/wandelroute/1311/Roots-Natuurpad-etappe-01/overzicht) (downloaded in November 2026), one per stage; the file for the whole route contains all 32. They have no elevation data, so there are no elevation profiles. The route and descriptions belong to Natuurmagazine Roots and Wandelnet.
