title: Veghel → Schijndel
start_transport: the Veghel railway embankment, corner of Rietven and Spoorven, at junction 62
---

Along forest paths and through the Wijboschbroek nature area to Schijndel. You may see storks and blackcaps.

**Note:**

- Around km 7.7 a culvert has been moved because of works: turn right along the Steegse Loop, cross the culvert, walk back along the other side and turn right to rejoin the route.

Route description, map and current route changes: [stage 28 on Wandelnet](https://www.wandelnet.nl/wandelroute/1353/overzicht).
