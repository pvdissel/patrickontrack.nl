title: Rolde → Papenvoort
start_transport: Het Dorp van Bartje museum, Balloërstraat 2, Rolde

Past dolmens and burial mounds, across a heath and past the Boomkroonpad treetop walk to Papenvoort. Look out for yellowhammers and four-spotted chasers.

Route description, map and current route changes: [stage 6 on Wandelnet](https://www.wandelnet.nl/wandelroute/1316/overzicht).
