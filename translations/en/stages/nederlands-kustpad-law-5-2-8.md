title: Wijk aan Zee → Egmond aan Zee
---

Nothing but dunes: through the Noordhollands Duinreservaat past Castricum and Egmond-Binnen, and over high grassy dunes to Egmond aan Zee.

**Note:**

- Halfway there's a link route to Castricum station.

Route description, map and current route changes: [stage 8 on Wandelnet](https://www.wandelnet.nl/wandelroute/992/overzicht).
