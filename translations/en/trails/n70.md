The N70 nature walk is a 14.2 km loop over eight “mountains” in the Rijk van Nijmegen, with names like the Boterberg, the Sterrenberg and the Duivelsberg. It runs through the rolling woods of Berg en Dal and Beek-Ubbergen, with views over the polders, the floodplains and the river landscape of the Waal and the Rhine. With about 375 metres of climbing and 500 steps, it's a favourite training ground for runners and long-distance walkers.

The route dates from 1970, European Nature Conservation Year; forester Theo Merkus was one of its founders. The printed route is sold at the Citystore Nijmegen, the VVV Groesbeek and Brasserie Charley, and is also online as a [leaflet](https://assets.plaece.nl/kuma-nijmegen/uploads/media/673c99ded9109/def-2022-n70-wandelroute.pdf).
