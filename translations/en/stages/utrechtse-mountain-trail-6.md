**Food and drink:** [Eetcafé De Remise](https://www.remiseleersum.nl/) (km 11.4, 600 m off the route), [Barbecue-restaurant De Heuvelrug](https://bbqleersum.nl/) (km 12.9).

**Places to stay:** [B&B Darthuizerpoort](https://www.darthuizerpoort.nl/) (km 3.5).
