Onto the Hondsrug ridge: through the Appèlbergen, with a pingo remnant, old cart tracks and drift sand, the Noordlaarderbos and the Vijftig Bunder heath to Zuidlaren. Look out for marsh gentian.

Route description, map and current route changes: [stage 4 on Wandelnet](https://www.wandelnet.nl/wandelroute/1314/overzicht).
