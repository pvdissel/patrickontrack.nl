From the forest pool at Leersum across the Den Berg and De Hoge Ginkel estates to the Amerongen woods.

**Food and drink:** [Barbecue-restaurant De Heuvelrug](https://bbqleersum.nl/) (km 0.0), [Restaurant Ginkeltuin](https://www.molecaten.nl/ginkeltuin) (km 1.9), [De Ossenberg](https://vakantieparkdeossenberg.nl/) (km 5.8), [Boshotel Overberg](https://www.boshoteloverberg.nl/) (km 6.7, 700 m off the route), [Restaurant Mas Montagne](https://masmontagne.nl/) (km 10.0).

**Places to stay:** [Molecaten Park Landgoed Ginkelduin](https://www.molecaten.nl/landgoed-ginkelduin/) (km 1.9), [Camping 't Eind](https://www.campingteind.nl/) (km 6.7, 700 m off the route), [Boshotel Overberg](https://www.boshoteloverberg.nl/) (km 6.7, 700 m off the route).

More about this stage, with a video and the route in Komoot: [stage 7 on utrechtsemountaintrail.nl](https://utrechtsemountaintrail.nl/etappe7/).
