title: Katwijk aan Zee → De Zilk
---

Past the fisherman's widow statue in Katwijk, through the Coepelduynen with the 1605 De Baak lighthouse behind you, and beyond Noordwijk past the bulb fields to De Zilk.

**Note:**

- Dogs are not allowed in the Amsterdamse Waterleidingduinen. With a dog, walk via Zandvoort and the beach to or from Langevelderslag.

Route description, map and current route changes: [stage 4 on Wandelnet](https://www.wandelnet.nl/wandelroute/996/overzicht).
