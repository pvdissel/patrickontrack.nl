I called that morning to check there was room for one small tent. After three hours of trains and a bus I walked in around 18:15, just as the farmer pulled up on his tractor. “Dutch Mountain Trail?” he asked, and walked me to the field: find yourself a nice spot, toilets on the right, and there’s a seating corner you can use.
