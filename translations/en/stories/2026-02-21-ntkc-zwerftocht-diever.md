On 21 February 2026 I walked the hike of the Wandering Group of the [Dutch Tourist Camping Club (NTKC)](https://www.ntkc.nl), in Diever.

It is a monthly event: on Saturday a route is set out with coordinates in the Dutch grid, leading you to an overnight stop. On Sunday you find your own way home.

The video is a silent hike: beautiful scenery and music, as if you are walking there yourself.
