The Nederlands Kustpad 2 (LAW 5-2) is the second part of the Dutch Coastal Path: 13 stages, about 246 km in all, from Hoek van Holland along the North Sea coast to the Afsluitdijk at Den Oever. You walk through hilly dunes on unpaved paths, past country estates on the edge of the dunes and the bulb fields, along the promenades of Katwijk and Egmond, through the parks of The Hague and the almshouses of Haarlem, with the beach always close. After Callantsoog the route leaves the coast and follows the dykes of the Kop van Noord-Holland and the former island of Wieringen to the Afsluitdijk.

## Stages and GPX

The GPX files come from [Wandelnet](https://www.wandelnet.nl/wandelroute/999/Nederlands-Kustpad-2/overzicht) (downloaded in November 2026), one per stage; the file for the whole route contains all 13. They have no elevation data. Wandelnet's file for stage 13 ran backwards; here it runs from Hippolytushoef to the Afsluitdijk. The variant [De Nollen – Den Helder](https://www.wandelnet.nl/wandelroute/983/overzicht) and the day walks [Wieringen](https://www.wandelnet.nl/wandelroute/985/overzicht) and [Den Helder](https://www.wandelnet.nl/wandelroute/984/overzicht) are on Wandelnet.
