This is an episode of my [TGO Challenge 2025](https://www.tgochallenge.com): solo, self-supported and on foot across Scotland. The plan, following the [Scottish National Trail](https://www.scottishnationaltrail.org.uk): from Poulary to Invergarry, 21 km and 300 m of ascent. The day was not over in Invergarry: the second part is in the next episode.
