title: Vaals → Gulpen
start_transport: Vaals bus station (parking at Vaalser Straße 549 or paid parking at the Three-Country Point)
---

Part 2 of the guidebook, about 23 km. The stage starts at the junction of Vaalser Straße and Keltenstraße in Vaalserquartier and climbs to the **Vaalserberg**, the highest point of the Netherlands. You then descend through the hills to Wahlwiller and climb to the top of the **Eyserbos**, the third of the Seven Summits. You finish in Gulpen, where the trail crosses the Rijksweg.

**Note:** at the church in Wahlwiller the trail crosses the churchyard. Please be respectful towards villagers visiting their loved ones.
