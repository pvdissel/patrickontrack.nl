title: Papenvoort → Hooghalen
start_transport: T-junction at marker stone 73, Rolderstraat (N857) between Papenvoort and Borger
---

Past small pools, over heath and through bog to Hooghalen, near the Westerbork Remembrance Centre and the radio telescopes. You may see common frogs, crossbills and red squirrels.

**Note:**

- Around km 10, just past marker PS 23166/FKP 58, beavers have dug a lodge under the cycle path. That bit is closed indefinitely: turn right at the T-junction and follow the alternative.
- Around km 13 the area is being re-wetted and the old route is hard going. Follow the new route (the GPX); guidebooks up to the 8th printing still show the old one.

Route description, map and current route changes: [stage 7 on Wandelnet](https://www.wandelnet.nl/wandelroute/1317/overzicht).
