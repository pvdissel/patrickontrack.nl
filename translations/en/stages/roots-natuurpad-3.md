Over grass paths and a wildlife corridor, between the Harkstederplas and Scharmerplas lakes, past the Westerbroek orchard and through the Oosterpolder and over the Onner Es to Glimmen.

Route description, map and current route changes: [stage 3 on Wandelnet](https://www.wandelnet.nl/wandelroute/1313/overzicht).
