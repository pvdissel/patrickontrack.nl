From Friday 3 to Sunday 5 October 2025 Milou and I went camping in Otterlo, at [Camping Beek en Hei](https://campingbeekenhei.nl), with rain (code yellow) in the forecast. On Saturday we went hiking and cycling, and visited Museonder in the [Hoge Veluwe National Park](https://hogeveluwe.nl).

This is our memory of this fun, cosy, wet and tough weekend.
