This is an episode of my [TGO Challenge 2025](https://www.tgochallenge.com): solo, self-supported and on foot across Scotland. In Invergarry I decided to walk on. The plan, following the [Scottish National Trail](https://www.scottishnationaltrail.org.uk): from Invergarry to Fort Augustus, 12 km and 100 m of ascent. The Sunday after was a rest day, by Loch Ness.
