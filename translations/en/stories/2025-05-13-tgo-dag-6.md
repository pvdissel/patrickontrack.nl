This is an episode of my [TGO Challenge 2025](https://www.tgochallenge.com): solo, self-supported and on foot across Scotland. The plan, following the [Scottish National Trail](https://www.scottishnationaltrail.org.uk): from Garva Bridge to Kingussie, 32 km and 400 m of ascent. As I had already walked on to Spey Dam the day before, I started there, and the day ended in Newtonmore.
