On Saturday 25 January 2025 my wife Elja ran the 10 km Night Trail of the [Houffa Trail](https://www.trailrun.be/houffatrail/) in Houffalize, Belgium. I shot some footage during the trail, and filmed the entire night trail route the next day. On the way I discovered that the day trails followed part of the 10 km night trail.

In the video the daytime and night footage alternate, so you get both the route and the atmosphere of the trail.
