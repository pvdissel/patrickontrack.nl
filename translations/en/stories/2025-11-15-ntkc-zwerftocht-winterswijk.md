On 15 November 2025 I walked the hike of the Wandering Group of the [Dutch Tourist Camping Club (NTKC)](https://www.ntkc.nl), in Winterswijk.

It is a monthly event: on Saturday a route is set out with coordinates in the Dutch grid, leading you to an overnight stop. On Sunday you find your own way home. More about the [Wandering Group](https://www.ntkc.nl/secties/zwerfgroep-2/) is on the NTKC website (in Dutch).
