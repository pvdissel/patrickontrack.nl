The Dutch Mountain Trail was laid out in 2020 by the Dutch Mountain Film Festival, for the festival's tenth edition and in the tradition of the *Jubiläumswege* in the Alps. It isn't the longest walk in the Netherlands, but it is the toughest: a rugged 101 km route linking the **Seven Summits** of Zuid-Limburg, from the Wilhelminaberg in Landgraaf to d'n Observant in Maastricht. On the way it also crosses the Vaalserberg, the highest point of the Netherlands.

The trail crosses the German and Belgian borders a few times, often without you noticing. Every summit looks out over a different valley: the Worm towards the Eifel, the Sinselbeek on the way from the Schneeberg to the Vaalserberg, the meandering Gulp and Geul, and finally the Voer region and the Meuse valley.

Don't underestimate it. Unpaved here means uneven cobbled paths and mud, miles of gentle climbs over windswept plateaus and steep, slippery descents through dark woods.

## Stages and GPX

The guidebook splits the trail into four parts of 28, 23, about 26.5 and 23.5 km.
