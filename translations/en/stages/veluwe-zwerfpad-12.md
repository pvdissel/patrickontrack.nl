title: Vaassen → Hoog Soeren
---

From Cannenburgh castle in Vaassen past spring-fed streams that drive watermills, through old forest with the rooting marks of wild boar, to Hoog Soeren and King William III's former hunting lodge.

**Note:**

- There's an unmarked alternative route through forest and heath, past the ‘Cathedral’ in the woods.

Route description, map and current route changes: [stage 12 on Wandelnet](https://www.wandelnet.nl/wandelroute/618/overzicht).
