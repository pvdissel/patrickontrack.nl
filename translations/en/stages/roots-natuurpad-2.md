From the Woldbrug along the Schildmeer and through the 't Roegwold nature area to Kolham. A long stage where you may see bearded reedlings, marsh harriers and kingfishers.

**Note:**

- In the guidebook up to the 8th printing this stage ends in Harkstede; it now ends in Kolham.
- Around km 6.4 the GPX goes straight on where the map in older guidebooks (1st to 6th printing) turns left. Follow the GPX: it's more unpaved.
- The boardwalk is now one-way. To walk it anyway, turn right at junction 39 around km 13 for an extra loop.

Route description, map and current route changes: [stage 2 on Wandelnet](https://www.wandelnet.nl/wandelroute/1312/overzicht).
