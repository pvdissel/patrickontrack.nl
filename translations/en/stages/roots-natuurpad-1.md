From the centre of Delfzijl along the Damsterdiep and the Eemskanaal to Appingedam, through the Groningen countryside where Highland cattle graze. On the way you pass the old Hijlkema brickworks and the Vliethoven manor.

**Note:**

- Around km 5.3 the grass path is closed at the end because of works. For now, follow the cycle path that bears right.

Route description, map and current route changes: [stage 1 on Wandelnet](https://www.wandelnet.nl/wandelroute/1311/overzicht).
