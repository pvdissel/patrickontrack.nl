title: Dieren → Rheden
---

A short stage past the Devil's Stone at De Steeg and over the Posbank in the Veluwezoom National Park, with views over the IJssel valley, to Rheden. The heather flowers in August.

Route description, map and current route changes: [stage 17 on Wandelnet](https://www.wandelnet.nl/wandelroute/622/overzicht).
