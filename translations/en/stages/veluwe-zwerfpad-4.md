title: Lunteren → Otterlo
---

From Lunteren to what was declared the centre of the Netherlands in 1930, then over the Wekeromse Zand, a fenced drift-sand area where mouflon keep the heath open, to Otterlo.

Route description, map and current route changes: [stage 4 on Wandelnet](https://www.wandelnet.nl/wandelroute/612/overzicht).
