title: Leuvenum → Elspeet

Along the Leuvenumse Beek, which further on is called the Staverdense and Hierdense Beek and once drove watermills, and past De Zandmolen to Elspeet.

Route description, map and current route changes: [stage 9 on Wandelnet](https://www.wandelnet.nl/wandelroute/1879/overzicht).
