title: Zuidlaren → Rolde
start_transport: Park and ride on Verlengde Stationsweg (N386), by the N34 bus stop, Zuidlaren
---

Through the Drentsche Aa stream valley and the Gasterse Duinen to Rolde. A big flock of Drenthe heath sheep grazes on the Balloërveld, and banded demoiselles fly along the stream.

Route description, map and current route changes: [stage 5 on Wandelnet](https://www.wandelnet.nl/wandelroute/1315/overzicht).
