Through the Amerongen woods, past the Lonely Oak and over the Elsterkop, one of the highest points of the Heuvelrug, to the old Kwintelooijen sand quarry between Veenendaal and Rhenen.

**Food and drink:** [Restaurant Mas Montagne](https://masmontagne.nl/) (km 0.6), [Residence Rhenen](https://www.residencerhenen.nl/restaurant/) (km 6.5), [House of Bird Kwintelooijen](https://www.houseofbird.nl/kwintelooijen/) (km 9.8).

**Places to stay:** [B&B De Blokhut](https://www.bed-en-breakfast.nl/benb/utrecht/elst/de-blokhut) (km 3.1, 500 m off the route).

More about this stage, with a video and the route in Komoot: [stage 8 on utrechtsemountaintrail.nl](https://utrechtsemountaintrail.nl/etappe8/).
