During our family holiday in Luxembourg we walked [route B2](https://www.mullerthal-trail.lu/en/fiche/walking/local-hiking-trail-b2) in the Mullerthal on 28 February 2025, 4.4 km, together with our 4-year-old daughter Milou. Highly recommended! For the parts in the carrier we brought the Rose & Rebellion Big Kid carrier, which was ideal.

We had rented a pod at [Camping Martbusch](https://camping-martbusch.lu/en/), right next to the route.
