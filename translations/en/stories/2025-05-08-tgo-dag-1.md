This is an episode of my [TGO Challenge 2025](https://www.tgochallenge.com): solo, self-supported and on foot across Scotland. And now it really begins. The plan, following the [Scottish National Trail](https://www.scottishnationaltrail.org.uk): from Shiel Bridge to Cluanie, 28 km and 740 m of ascent.
