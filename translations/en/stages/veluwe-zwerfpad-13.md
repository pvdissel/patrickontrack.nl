title: Hoog Soeren → Kootwijk
---

Along wide paths through the Crown Estate and past the Asselsche Heide to the still lake in the Kootwijkerveen and Kootwijk.

**Note:**

- During the deer rut you take a different route; there's also an alternative to a stretch of cycle path.

Route description, map and current route changes: [stage 13 on Wandelnet](https://www.wandelnet.nl/wandelroute/619/overzicht).
