title: Wolfheze → Planken Wambuis
---

Over the Jac. P. Thijsse wildlife bridge, past Veluwe spring-fed streams, the Papermakers' monument and the listed Everwijnsgoed farm in the Renkum stream valley, and past the Nivon house De Bosbeek to Planken Wambuis. On the way are a Bell Beaker burial mound and Celtic fields.

Route description, map and current route changes: [stage 2 on Wandelnet](https://www.wandelnet.nl/wandelroute/1874/overzicht).
