On 2 January 2025 I walked a section of the [StauSeeSteig](https://www.dmff.eu/stauseesteig/), through a winter wonderland with snow and icy winds. The plan was to walk the whole StauSeeSteig from 1 January, but railway problems caused by the winter weather delayed it by a day, and in the end I walked two days: from Langerwehe station, with a night in the tent at the Wehebachtalsperre, to Zerkall station.

The video is a silent hike: beautiful scenery and music, as if you are walking there yourself.
