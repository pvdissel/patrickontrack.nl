title: N70 Natuurwandelroute
start_transport: Brasserie Charley, Nieuwe Holleweg 4, or one of the five other starting points
---

The whole 14.2 km loop over eight ‘mountains’ in Berg en Dal, Beek-Ubbergen and Nijmegen, with about 375 metres of climbing and 500 steps. You can start at six places:

1. Brasserie Charley, Nieuwe Holleweg 4, Beek-Ubbergen
2. Bus stop for line 58, junction of Rijksstraatweg and the N325, Beek-Ubbergen
3. De Duivelsberg / Holdeurn car park, Oude Kleefsebaan (near no. 429), Berg en Dal
4. De Heksendans pancake house, Oude Kleefsebaan 425, Berg en Dal
5. Fletcher Hotel Val Monte, Oude Holleweg 5, Berg en Dal
6. Restaurant Tante Koosje, Berg en Dalseweg 359, Nijmegen

On the way: the Duivelsberg with the Great and Little Motte and a view over the Wylerbergmeer, the German border with its stone markers, the Kleverberg, the Filosofenbeek, the woods of the Heerlijkheid Beek with the Stollenberg and Boterberg, the Hengstdal, the Ravenberg and the Kastanjedal. There's a water tap on the Rijksstraatweg.

At De Heksendans (start 4) you can cut the route short by following the Elzenbeek down to the village. The GPX starts at the De Duivelsberg car park.

The full route description is on [Visit Nijmegen](https://www.visitnijmegen.com/routes/3673389898/n70-natuurwandelroute).
