Over heath and forest paths with sundew and cross-leaved heath to Dwingeloo. Adders and moor frogs live here.

**Note:**

- Around km 9.4 the route is closed; follow the diversion.

Route description, map and current route changes: [stage 9 on Wandelnet](https://www.wandelnet.nl/wandelroute/1319/overzicht).
