During our family holiday in Sweden I went out alone early in the morning of 17 August 2025, looking for moose around Orsa Grönklitt. I walked [Rädsjön runt](https://www.visitdalarna.se/en/do/activities/radsjon-runt-5-km), a 5 km loop around the lake.

The video is a silent hike: beautiful scenery and music, as if you are walking there yourself.
