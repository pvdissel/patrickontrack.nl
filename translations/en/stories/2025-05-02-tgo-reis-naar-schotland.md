This is an episode of my [TGO Challenge 2025](https://www.tgochallenge.com): solo, self-supported and on foot across Scotland. Before I could start, I first had to get there: some 1500 km from the Netherlands to Shiel Bridge by boat, train and bus. On Friday the boat to Newcastle, on Saturday the train to Inverness, on Sunday a walk around Inverness and on Monday on to Shiel Bridge.

## In the video

- **00:00** Travel to TGO Challenge
- **00:14** Friday: Ferry from The Netherlands to Newcastle, England
- **05:13** Saturday: Train from Newcastle, England to Inverness, Scotland
- **09:53** Sunday: Walk around Inverness
- **13:36** Monday: Journey to Shiel Bridge
- **19:38** Exploring the campsite upstream
