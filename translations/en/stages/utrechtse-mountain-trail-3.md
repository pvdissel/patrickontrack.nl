From the plain of the Treekerheide past the Pyramid of Austerlitz to the Koeheuvels in Maarn.

**Food and drink:** [Pannenkoekenhuis Bergzicht](https://pannenkoekenhuizen.com/bergzicht/) (km 0.2), [Restaurant De Pyramide van Austerlitz](https://www.pyramidevanausterlitz.nl/horeca/) (km 3.0, 300 m off the route).

**Places to stay:** [NTKC Austerlitz](https://www.ntkc.nl/onze-terreinen/austerlitz/) (km 6.8, 500 m off the route), [Buitenplaats De Bergse Bossen](https://www.bergsebossen.nl/) (km 6.8, 2.1 km off the route).

More about this stage, with a video and the route in Komoot: [stage 3 on utrechtsemountaintrail.nl](https://utrechtsemountaintrail.nl/etappe3/).
