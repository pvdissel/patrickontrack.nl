From Arnhem station through Park Sonsbeek, laid out around the Sint-Jansbeek, then along the Bosbeek and the Renkum stream valley to the big forests at Wolfheze. On the way: burial mounds on Warnsborn, the Wodan oaks and the Wolfhezerheide wildlife bridge.

Route description, map and current route changes: [stage 1 on Wandelnet](https://www.wandelnet.nl/wandelroute/611/overzicht).
