Along quiet forest paths and heath, then through the Het Loo Crown Estate to Apeldoorn. Look out for redstarts and black woodpeckers.

Route description, map and current route changes: [stage 18 on Wandelnet](https://www.wandelnet.nl/wandelroute/1339/overzicht).
