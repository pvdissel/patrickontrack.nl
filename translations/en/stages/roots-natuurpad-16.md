start_transport: Wijhe station

Past a wild-plant garden to the IJssel, which you cross on the Wijhe–Vorchten ferry. Then along the Apeldoorns Kanaal and across the Vosbergen estate to Heerde.

**Note:**

- This stage needs the ferry across the IJssel. Check that it's running before you set off.

Route description, map and current route changes: [stage 16 on Wandelnet](https://www.wandelnet.nl/wandelroute/1326/overzicht).
