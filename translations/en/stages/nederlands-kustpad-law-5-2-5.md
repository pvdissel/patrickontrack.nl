title: De Zilk → Haarlem station
---

Through the Amsterdamse Waterleidingduinen, with a good chance of roe deer and fallow deer, and past Kraantje Lek to Haarlem station.

**Note:**

- Dogs are not allowed in the Amsterdamse Waterleidingduinen. Around km 12 there's an alternative route for walkers with a dog.

Route description, map and current route changes: [stage 5 on Wandelnet](https://www.wandelnet.nl/wandelroute/995/overzicht).
