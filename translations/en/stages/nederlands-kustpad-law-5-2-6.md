title: Haarlem station → Santpoort-Noord
---

Through Haarlem's historic centre, past the St Bavo church, and in a wide arc through the Zuid-Kennemerland National Park, with high dunes and old oaks, to Santpoort-Noord.

Route description, map and current route changes: [stage 6 on Wandelnet](https://www.wandelnet.nl/wandelroute/994/overzicht).
