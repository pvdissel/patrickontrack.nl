Part 3 of the guidebook, about 26 km. Right after crossing the Rijksweg in Gulpen you start the climb up the Gulperberg. Through the hills south of Gulpen, almost to the Belgian border, you reach the **Kattenroth** near Noorbeek, the sixth of the Seven Summits. The stage ends in Mheer.

**Note:**

- On the car park after the Rijksweg crossing there is a new ambulance station, right on the trail. Walk round it to the left: the path up the hill starts behind the building.
- In Mheer, don't park on Dorpsstraat (it bothers the residents) but at the car park on Burgemeester Beckersweg.
