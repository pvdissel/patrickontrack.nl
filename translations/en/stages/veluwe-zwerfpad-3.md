title: Planken Wambuis → Lunteren

Through Het Oude Hout, a quiet zone for red deer, roe deer and wild boar, past veteran trees and the De Mossel tea house, and over the Wekeromse Zand to Lunteren.

Route description, map and current route changes: [stage 3 on Wandelnet](https://www.wandelnet.nl/wandelroute/1880/overzicht).
