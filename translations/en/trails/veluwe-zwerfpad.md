The Veluwe Zwerfpad is a loop of about 287 km in 19 stages, from Arnhem station and back. You walk mostly on unpaved paths through the Veluwe's big drift-sand areas, heaths and rolling forests, home to red deer, roe deer, wild boar and mouflon.

The loop goes from Arnhem via Wolfheze and Lunteren to Otterlo, Kootwijk and Garderen, through the Speulderbos to Putten, over the heath to Elspeet, Vierhouten and Vaassen, then south past Hoog Soeren, Kootwijk, Hoenderloo, Loenen and Dieren and over the Posbank back to Arnhem.

## Stages and GPX

The GPX files come from [Wandelnet](https://www.wandelnet.nl/wandelroute/611/Veluwe-Zwerfpad/overzicht) (downloaded in November 2026), one per stage; the file for the whole route contains all 19. They have no elevation data. The six shortcuts aren't stages here, but they're on Wandelnet: [20 Garderen – Elspeet](https://www.wandelnet.nl/wandelroute/626/overzicht), [21 Elspeet – Spaanse Kappen](https://www.wandelnet.nl/wandelroute/627/overzicht), [22 Hoog Soeren – Garderen](https://www.wandelnet.nl/wandelroute/628/overzicht), [23 Schaarsbergen – Otterlo](https://www.wandelnet.nl/wandelroute/623/overzicht), [24 Otterlo – Hoenderloo](https://www.wandelnet.nl/wandelroute/624/overzicht) and [25 Hoenderloo – Schaarsbergen](https://www.wandelnet.nl/wandelroute/625/overzicht).
