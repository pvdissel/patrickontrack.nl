From Amersfoort station through the Bergkwartier and Klein Zwitserland neighbourhoods, past the Belgian Monument and Camp Amersfoort, to the car park by Maranatha cemetery on the edge of Leusden.

**Food and drink:** [Bergpaviljoen](https://www.bergpaviljoen.nl/) (km 3.3), [Fletcher Hotel-Restaurant Amersfoort](https://www.fletcherhotelamersfoort.nl) (km 6.0), [Restaurant Zuiver](https://www.restaurantzuiver.nl/amersfoort/) (km 11.0).

**Places to stay:** [Fletcher Hotel-Restaurant Amersfoort](https://www.fletcherhotelamersfoort.nl) (km 6.0), [Landgoed ISVW](https://www.landgoedisvw.nl/kamers) (km 11.2, 800 m off the route).

More about this stage, with a video and the route in Komoot: [stage 1 on utrechtsemountaintrail.nl](https://utrechtsemountaintrail.nl/etappe1/).
