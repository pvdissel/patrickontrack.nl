Part 1 of the guidebook, about 28 km. From Eygelshoven station you climb straight up the **Wilhelminaberg**, the first of the Seven Summits. The trail then passes Erenstein castle and runs a long way through Germany (Orsbach) to the **Schneeberg**, the second summit. The stage ends at the junction of Vaalser Straße and Keltenstraße in Vaalserquartier, on the edge of Vaals.

**Route changes** (since the guidebook came out, according to the DMFF):

- The climb up the Wilhelminaberg has changed and the description of the descent is clearer.
- Between the Brughofweg and Erenstein castle you now cross the water at a new spot.
- Between the Schneeberg and Vaals the owner has closed off the field the trail used to cross. Go round it to the left: follow Schurzelterstraße for a bit and after 300 m turn right into Vaalserstraße.

The updated maps are PDFs on the [official page](https://www.dmff.eu/dutch-mountain-trail/#opmerkingen).
