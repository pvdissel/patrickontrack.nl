The Dutch Mountain Trail was created for the Dutch Mountain Film Festival, in the tradition of the anniversary trails in the Alps. It links the walks around the “Seven Summits” of Zuid-Limburg into one long route of about 100 km, with more climbing than any other long-distance walk in the Netherlands.
