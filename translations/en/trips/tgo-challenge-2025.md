In May 2025 I embarked on my biggest adventure in a long time: solo hiking [The Great Outdoors (TGO) Challenge](https://www.tgochallenge.com). This is a community event where all participants travel self-supported, on foot, across Scotland, from the west coast to the east coast, on a route they put together themselves. A large part of my route followed the [Scottish National Trail](https://www.scottishnationaltrail.org.uk).

Two weeks too long, or still in doubt? Try the [TGO Challenge East](https://www.tgochallenge.com/tgoceast/).
