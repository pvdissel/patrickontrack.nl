title: Ockenburgh → Den Haag Centraal
---

Through the 350-year-old Ockenburgh estate, with rhododendrons in May, via Kijkduin, Scheveningen and the Scheveningse Bosjes to Den Haag Centraal.

**Note:**

- Around km 13 there's an alternative route for walkers with a dog.

Route description, map and current route changes: [stage 2 on Wandelnet](https://www.wandelnet.nl/wandelroute/998/overzicht).
