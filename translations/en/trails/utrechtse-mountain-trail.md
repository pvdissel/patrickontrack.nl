The Utrechtse Mountain Trail runs in nine stages over the Utrechtse Heuvelrug, from Amersfoort station to Rhenen station: according to its makers about 96 km with more than 1,200 metres of climbing, "uphill in the flat Netherlands". It passes the Pyramid of Austerlitz, the Maarnse Berg, the Elsterkop and the Grebbeberg, among others, and the last stage is the hardest.

The stages are short, about 10 to 13 km, so they're easy to walk one at a time. If you want to walk the whole trail in one go, the [Doorlopers](https://utrechtsemountaintrail.nl/doorlopers/) page suggests two- and three-day schedules with hotels or campsites.

## Stages and GPX

The GPX files come from [utrechtsemountaintrail.nl](https://utrechtsemountaintrail.nl), one per stage and one for the whole trail (downloaded in November 2026). The distances here come from those files and the climbing per stage from the site; together that's about 101 km, a bit more than the 96 km the site mentions. Questions or news about the trail: the [UMT group on Facebook](https://www.facebook.com/groups/UtrechtseMountainTrail/).
