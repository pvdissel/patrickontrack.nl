This is an episode of my [TGO Challenge 2025](https://www.tgochallenge.com): solo, self-supported and on foot across Scotland. Before the start, I walked from the campsite in Glenshiel up the Five Sisters of Kintail for the view, and back again. My summit was between Sgùrr na Moraich and Beinn Bhuidhe.

- **05:52** Reached my summit, between Sgurr na Moraich and Beinn Bhuidhe
