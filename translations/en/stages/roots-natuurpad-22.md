Through the woods at Doorwerth to the Nederrijn, which you cross, and over the Schoonderlogt estate to Valburg. You may see toads and storks.

Route description, map and current route changes: [stage 22 on Wandelnet](https://www.wandelnet.nl/wandelroute/1344/overzicht).
